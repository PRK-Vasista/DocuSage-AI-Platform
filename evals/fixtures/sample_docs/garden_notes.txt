Garden Notes — March planting guide. Tomatoes need six hours of sun and
well-drained soil. Basil prefers warm nights above 15 degrees Celsius.
Water deeply twice a week unless rainfall exceeds 20 millimeters.

Do not plant potatoes beside tomatoes. Carrots and onions may share a bed.
The community greenhouse opens at 08:00 on Saturdays. Tools must be returned
to the shed labeled North Wing.
