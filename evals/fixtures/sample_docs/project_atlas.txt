Project Atlas ships three modules: Intake, Review, and Archive. Intake accepts
PDF and DOCX uploads only. Review assigns each file to a human owner within
24 hours. Archive stores approved files for seven years.

The Atlas SLA for first response is four hours on business days. Escalations
go to the on-call engineer named Maya Chen. The maximum concurrent Review
workers is twelve. Atlas does not process video files in version 2.1.
